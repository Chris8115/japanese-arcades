# MUSIC DIVER

MUSIC DIVER joins the Japanese Arcades creative tab and Arcade Ordering Kiosk
as `japanesearcades:music_diver`.

The supplied photographs guide the white wedge-shaped chassis, steeply inclined
circular play screen, four separate black arc pads, cyan pad markings, and pink
perimeter lights. A smaller rectangular status screen continues uphill behind
the play surface. The upright round marquee has four illuminated circuit sectors,
diagonal dividers, a central logo and an angular pink backing. Two speakers,
front service doors, a cyan reader and a long stick holster complete the cabinet.

## Obtain and use

Order it for **18 iron ingots, 4 gold ingots, 1 diamond, 8 glass and 8 redstone**,
or select it from the Japanese Arcades creative tab:

```text
/give @s japanesearcades:music_diver
```

It occupies one supported column, three blocks high. Its visible dimensions are
approximately **1.25 blocks wide × 1.14 deep × 2.98 high**. Allow extra clearance
around the projecting play surface and broad marquee. Every section shares one
saved music-disc inventory; breaking a section removes the connected cabinet.

Right-click with an Arcade Pass for the existing simulated rank and Prize Point
system. The default cost is 10 Credits; machine overrides use
`japanesearcades:music_diver`. Insert and eject music discs using the same
right-click interactions as the other music cabinets. The displayed rhythm UI
and strike pads are decorative.

Circuit patterns, labels and screen UI are generated by the model exporter.
The cabinet uses static native OBJ geometry and cached collision and selection
shapes, without a per-frame cabinet renderer.
The optimized exports contain **2,289 world faces** across the three sections
and **2,122 item faces**. The twelve part/facing shapes are cached once; aiming
at the rings does not rebuild their collision geometry.

Regenerate the model using `python scripts/make_music_diver_model.py`, then run
`python scripts/optimize_models.py --write`. Studio previews and the packed
Blender scene use `scripts/render_music_diver_model.py`. Normal builds use the
checked-in resources and do not require Python or Blender.

Restart the development client to load the new cabinet registration. F3+T can
reload its models and textures after registration.
