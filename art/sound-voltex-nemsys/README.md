# SOUND VOLTEX NEMSYS cabinet

The original NEMSYS-style cabinet joins the Japanese Arcades creative tab and
the Arcade Ordering Kiosk's Machines category as `japanesearcades:sound_voltex_nemsys`.
Its EXCEED GEAR styling follows the supplied cabinet photographs: four tapered
angular wings around a narrow portrait monitor, square black side speakers,
magenta and cyan circuit lighting, a metallic SOUND VOLTEX / EXCEED GEAR marquee, and a separate
illustrated topper. The black pedestal has service doors and a silver foot.

The projecting control deck carries two rotary knobs, four white BT keys and
two dark FX keys with pink surrounds. A small angled card-reader panel sits
behind the controls. These controls are decorative; the cabinet supports the
same Arcade Pass simulated plays and saved music-disc playback as the other
rhythm cabinets.

## Obtaining and using it

Order it for **16 iron ingots, 4 gold ingots, 1 diamond, 6 glass, and 6 redstone**,
or select it in the Japanese Arcades creative tab:

```text
/give @s japanesearcades:sound_voltex_nemsys
```

Place it on a solid floor with three clear vertical blocks. Leave side clearance
for the light wings and speakers; two-block center spacing separates neighboring
cabinets. Breaking any section removes the cabinet and drops one item in survival.
The visible model measures approximately **1.37 blocks wide, 2.96 high, and
1.09 deep**. Dimensions are estimated from the photographs.

Right-click with an Arcade Pass to spend Credits and receive a simulated rank
and Prize Points. The shared default is 10 Credits per play; the server economy
configuration accepts `japanesearcades:sound_voltex_nemsys` for machine overrides.
Right-click with a music disc to insert it; right-click again to eject it. Each
cabinet keeps its own disc and playback progress.

The illustrations were created with the built-in imagegen tool as original art
inspired by the references. Source pixels are preserved; the runtime packer only
pads the canvases to texture dimensions. Circuit patterns, labels, and trim are
generated by the model exporter.

The model uses static chunk-rendered indexed OBJ geometry with emissive screen
and light faces. It adds no per-frame cabinet renderer or client ticker.
Collision and selection shapes are cached for every section and facing.
The optimized item has **809 faces**; the three world sections total **900 faces**
after clipping the mesh at block boundaries.
Vanilla shows the illuminated colors; bloom depends on the active shader pack.

Regenerate with `python scripts/make_nemsys_model.py`, followed by
`python scripts/optimize_models.py --write`. Blender previews use
`scripts/render_nemsys_model.py`. Normal builds use the checked-in assets and
do not require Python or Blender. Restart the development client after installing
a build that adds this cabinet; F3+T only reloads existing models and textures.
