JAPANESE ARCADES - GALLERY SELECTION

Start with 01-recommended-gallery: 16 actual Minecraft screenshots, numbered
in a suggested upload order. For a shorter gallery, use 01, 02, 04, 05, 06,
07, 08, 09, 11, and 14, then add your favorite cabinet close-ups.

02-cabinet-closeups contains 21 optional views covering all 22 cabinet designs.
Nostalgia and BeatStream share one image showing their corrected control panels.
The jubeat image is a studio render showing its current gray pads; label it
as a model render when uploading it.
03-extra-views contains the building interior, murals, glow sticks, custom
display boxes, and villager trades. Shader screenshots are identified in their
captions; keep those labels so viewers know the lighting uses a shader pack.

Open index.html to browse the images and their captions. Full-resolution PNGs
can be uploaded individually. Suggested titles and captions are also below.

The gallery preserves the original image pixels. Unnecessary file metadata has
been removed for publication. There are 42
in-game screenshots and one clearly labeled jubeat studio model render.
No AI restyling, retouching, cropping, resizing, or new screenshot capture was
performed. The actual images and dimensions are recorded in manifest.json.
UI captures use demonstration inventories and balances; configurable prices
and stock can differ in players' worlds.
